We want people to find these resources, including through search engines and AI assistants. We ask that you do so in a way that sends them to us.

## What we welcome

Search engines and AI assistants that read a page to answer a person's question, quote a short passage, and link to the source. Our `robots.txt` allows these crawlers, and our `llms.txt` file lists the pages worth citing.

## What we ask you not to do

Do not use the text, images, audio or video on this site to train, fine-tune or evaluate machine learning models. We reserve our rights in text and data mining. This is stated in our `robots.txt`, in a `tdm-reservation` signal on every page and in `/.well-known/tdmrep.json`.

Scripture quotations are from the King James Version, which is in the public domain. Our own writing, recordings and designs are not.

## Please note

These signals are requests that responsible companies honour. If you want to license our content for a purpose, please get in touch through the links in the footer.
