A circle is a few friends, a guide and an hour. You do not need a leader or any training. You need a table and a willingness to be honest.

## Before you meet

1. Invite two to six friends. Smaller groups talk more.
2. Pick a guide from the [Read](/read/) page. Each one takes about an hour.
3. Print it with the Print button on the guide, or open it on a phone.

## When you meet

1. **Read the passage out loud**, taking turns, one verse each. Read it twice if you like.
2. **Talk about what the passage says**. The guide gives a short explanation to start you off.
3. **Tell each other**. Each guide has a question that asks for a story. Everyone answers, including the person who is quiet.
4. **Pray together**. Thank God for one thing you noticed in someone else, and ask for what each of you needs.

## A few ground rules

- What is said in the circle stays in the circle.
- No one has to answer every question.
- It is fine not to know. Write the question down and look at it again next week.

## Keep going

When you finish a guide, pick the next one in the same [project](/projects/), or listen to an episode on the same theme.
