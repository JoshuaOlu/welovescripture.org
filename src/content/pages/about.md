We Love Scripture began as a podcast of Bible devotions. It has grown into something wider: a group of friends making resources that other friends can use to read Scripture together.

## What we make

- **Guides** for a circle of friends to work through together. They teach something from Scripture and ask you to tell each other a story.
- **Articles** to read on your own and talk over later.
- **Videos** on YouTube that start a conversation.
- **A podcast** of short episodes that teach the Bible and encourage a deeper faith.
- **Resources for children**, as we are able.

## What we believe about reading together

Knowing God happens in Scripture, and it happens in company. When friends read, listen and pray side by side, they learn about God and they learn about each other. That is the heart of everything here.

::: talk
Tell each other about a time you needed someone to help you up. What did their help show you about God?
:::
